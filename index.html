Know who owes you, what is overdue, and how much of each payment to put aside for tax. One HTML file you open in your browser. Works offline. No account, no subscription, no tracking.

What it does

  • Invoices. Log client, description, issue date, terms and amount. Due date, status (Open / Paid / Overdue) and days overdue are calculated for you; overdue invoices are highlighted. Mark paid in one click.
  • Printable invoices. Print or save any invoice as a PDF with your business details and payment instructions.
  • Expenses. Vendor, category (your own list), deductible flag, totals by category.
  • Dashboard. Received, expenses, profit, outstanding and overdue totals, plus a 12-month table and quarter totals for the year you pick.
  • Tax set-aside. Choose a percentage; see how much of every payment, month and quarter to move to savings. (A budgeting aid, not tax advice.)
  • Clients. Invoiced, received, outstanding, overdue and average days-to-pay per client.
  • Backup and export. JSON backup and restore on any device; CSV export for Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets.

Honest limitations

  • One currency label and one line item per invoice.
  • No automatic sync between devices: move data with the backup file.
  • No bank import.
  • Data lives in your browser's local storage; clearing site data without a backup deletes it.

FAQ

Does it send my data anywhere? No. It makes no network requests and loads no external scripts or fonts.

Which browsers? Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop.

Is the tax figure accurate for my country? It is simply the percentage you choose times what you received. Ask a qualified accountant which rate fits you.

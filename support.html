Support & refunds

Getting help

  • Order, download or payment questions and refund requests: reply to the receipt email from Polar or use the Polar customer portal linked in it. Polar is the merchant of record and can process refunds directly.
  • Product questions and bug reports: open an issue in this site's public GitHub repository (link in the footer of GitHub Pages, or search the organization for small-tools-studio). Please do not post personal or financial data in a public issue.

Support requests are read and handled by the AI agent that runs this store, with its owner able to step in. Replies are not instant.

Refund policy

If a product does not work for you or does not match its description, ask for a refund within 30 days of purchase and it will be refunded in full. No need to justify it in detail.

Delivery

Files are delivered instantly by Polar after payment, on the confirmation page and by email.

Privacy

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. Checkout data is collected only by Polar for payment and tax purposes. The products themselves make no network requests.

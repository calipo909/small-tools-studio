Support & refunds

Getting help

For order, download or payment questions, product questions, bug reports and refund requests, reply to the receipt email from Polar or send a message through the Polar customer portal linked in that email.

These requests go to the store's owner (a person), not to the AI agent. The AI agent that builds the products and this website has no inbox and does not read support messages. The owner can answer questions and issue refunds. Replies are not instant.

Please don't post personal or financial data anywhere public.

Refund policy

If a product does not work for you or does not match its description, ask for a refund within 30 days of purchase and it will be refunded in full. No need to justify it in detail.

Delivery

Files are delivered instantly by Polar after payment, on the confirmation page and by email.

Privacy

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. Checkout data is collected only by Polar for payment and tax purposes. The products themselves make no network requests.

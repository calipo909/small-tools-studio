Free proposal follow-up email templates for freelancers

Sent a quote and heard nothing? Three short follow-ups, from gentle to closing the loop. Replace the [brackets]. Free to use for your own business, no signup.

Tips: reply in the same thread so the proposal is one scroll away, keep each follow-up under 80 words, and give the client an easy way to say "not now". Silence usually means busy, not no.

1. Gentle check-in (3-5 days after sending)

Subject: Re: Proposal: [project name]

Hi [Name],

Just checking whether you had a chance to look at the proposal.
Happy to answer questions or adjust the scope if anything doesn't
fit your budget or timeline.

Best,
[Your name]

2. Useful nudge (about a week later)

Subject: Re: Proposal: [project name]

Hi [Name],

One thought since my proposal: [a specific, useful idea or answer to
a question they raised, in one or two sentences].

If the timing works, I can start on [date]. If it's easier to discuss,
here are two slots for a short call: [time 1], [time 2] ([timezone]).

Best,
[Your name]

3. Close the loop (10-14 days after sending)

Subject: Should I close this out?

Hi [Name],

I haven't heard back, so I'm guessing timing or priorities have
changed - totally fine. I'll assume we're not moving ahead for now
and release the time I'd reserved.

If things change, just reply to this email and we can pick it up.

Best,
[Your name]

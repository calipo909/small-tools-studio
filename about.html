About Small Tools Studio

Small Tools Studio makes small, practical digital tools: single-file apps and templates you own outright, with no accounts or subscriptions.

Who runs it: this business is operated by an AI agent acting on behalf of its owner. The AI agent designs, writes and tests the products, writes this website, and handles support. The products are not made by a named human expert, and nothing here is professional financial, legal or tax advice.

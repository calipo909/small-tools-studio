Free rate increase email templates for freelancers

Three polite ways to tell clients your rates are going up. Replace the [brackets]. Free to use for your own business, no signup.

Tips: give 30-60 days' notice, state the old and new rate plainly, keep the reason to one sentence, and say what happens to work already agreed. Don't apologise or over-explain.

1. Standard notice

Subject: Update to my rates from [date]

Hi [Name],

I've really valued working with you on [work].

From [date], my rate for [service] will change from [old rate] to
[new rate]. This reflects [one-sentence reason, e.g. increased demand
and expanded skills].

Work already agreed before [date] stays at the current rate.

Happy to talk it through if you have questions.

Best,
[Your name]

2. With a grace period for a long-standing client

Subject: My rates for [year]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for [length of time] of working together.

My standard rate for new clients is now [new rate]. Because you've
been with me since [year], your current rate of [old rate] will stay
in place until [date]. From then on it will be [new rate or loyalty
rate].

If it helps with budgeting, I'm happy to agree a fixed quote for
[upcoming project] at today's rate before [date].

Best,
[Your name]

3. New projects only

Subject: Quick note on pricing for new projects

Hi [Name],

A short heads-up: from [date], new projects will be quoted at
[new rate / new package price]. Anything we've already scoped,
including [current project], stays at the agreed price.

Let me know if you'd like to lock in [next piece of work] before then.

Best,
[Your name]

Free scope creep email templates for freelancers

Three polite replies for "can you just add..." requests. Replace the [brackets]. Free to use for your own business, no signup.

Tips: answer quickly and warmly, name the original scope in one line, and always give the client a choice instead of a flat no. Put cost and date impact in writing before doing the extra work.

1. Offer two options (the default reply)

Subject: Re: [their request]

Hi [Name],

Good idea - [one sentence on why it makes sense].

This is outside the original scope ([brief scope]), so here are
two ways to handle it:

1. Add it now: about [X hours/days], [amount], and final delivery
   moves to [date].
2. Phase 2: we finish the current scope on schedule, then do this
   right after.

Which would you prefer? Once you confirm, I'll send an updated
estimate.

Best,
[Your name]

2. Small request you're happy to absorb (once)

Subject: Re: [their request]

Hi [Name],

Happy to include [small change] at no extra cost this time.

Just so we're aligned for the rest of the project: anything beyond
[the agreed scope / remaining revision rounds] I'll quote
separately before starting, so there are no surprises on the
invoice.

Best,
[Your name]

3. Requests keep piling up

Subject: Quick check-in on scope and timeline

Hi [Name],

Over the last [weeks], we've added [list of 2-3 requests] on top
of the original plan. I want to make sure the timeline and budget
still work for you.

Could we take 15 minutes to agree which of these are must-haves
for this phase? I'll then send a short updated plan with cost and
dates, and park the rest for a phase 2.

Best,
[Your name]
